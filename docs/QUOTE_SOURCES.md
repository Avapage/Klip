# Quote sources

Klip displays 100 brief traditional sayings in English, grouped by broad traditions rather than claiming a single author or exclusive place of origin. Proverbs often circulate across languages; some wording is shortened or lightly modernized.

Reference works include [Arabian Wisdom](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/33109) by John Wortabet, [Proverb Lore](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/34176) by F. Edward Hulme, [Proverbs of All Nations](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/63190) by Walter K. Kelly, [China Revolutionized](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/57247) by John Stuart Thomson, and [Barkham Burroughs' Encyclopaedia](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/14091). These historical collections are in the public domain in the United States; individual proverbs can have multiple traditions or disputed origins. Other widely circulating sayings are labeled simply “Traditional sayings.”
